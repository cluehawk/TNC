MANUAL PLAYTEST CHECKLIST
=========================

Automated baseline: run BUILD\TEST.BAT and confirm all core regression tests pass.

1. Start the program and place each prisoner's Start tile in any empty board space.
2. Complete all four opening turns and confirm connected opening tiles generate.
3. Leave an area behind and confirm unlit unoccupied tiles vanish.
   Confirm a disconnected adjacent tile remains when it visibly blocks an open passage.
   Aim two candles into one empty space and confirm the placed tile connects to both.
4. Find or force a crumbling tile. Enter it, then move away and confirm it becomes a pit.
5. Stay on an armed crumbling tile and confirm the prisoner begins falling.
6. On the falling prisoner's next turn, try a valid and an invalid re-entry edge.
   With an exhausted stack and an empty landing space, confirm re-entry is impossible.
   When the selected row/column is full, confirm an existing tile can be selected.
7. Move into line of sight of a monster and confirm lights-out plus draw loss.
   Connect two wax eaters, trigger one, and confirm each attacks only once.
   Confirm the full chain resolves before darkness removes extinguished tiles.
   Scramble onto a Key or crumble tile and confirm its entry effect resolves.
   Scramble onto another wax eater and confirm the second attack resolves before another scramble.
   Draw a wax eater while Staying and confirm it replaces the target with a 4-way monster tile.
8. Move a lit prisoner adjacent to a lights-out prisoner and confirm relight.
   Repeat by moving the lights-out prisoner beside the lit prisoner.
   Confirm the newly relit candle immediately reveals its complete radius.
   While still lights out, move into an empty space and confirm only that destination is drawn.
   Spend nerve to Stay while lights out and confirm the spent nerve is not regained.
9. Spend nerve to Move Again and confirm Stay is not offered as the extra action.
   Stay with no nerve and confirm the earned nerve is unavailable for Move Again.
   In Final Flickers, confirm that newly earned nerve cannot Sustain that same turn.
   Confirm the nerve is added only after the flicker and is available next turn.
10. Continue until draws reach zero and confirm final flickers begin.
11. Spend nerve to cancel a final flicker, then skip the spend and select which eligible lit tile is removed.
    Confirm occupied tiles are protected while an unoccupied lit tile exists.
    If only occupied lit tiles remain, confirm one is removed and the game is lost.
    Remove a connecting tile and confirm the game ends if no single Gate remains
    reachable by all four Prisoners.
12. Carry a Key across another Key tile and confirm the unclaimed Key remains on the armed tile.
    Pass the held Key while still there and confirm the freed hand claims the tile's Key.
13. Gather keys and stack all four keyed prisoners on a gate to confirm victory.
